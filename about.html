About

I’m a postdoctoral fellow at Genentech and Stanford, working with Aviv Regev and Jure Leskovec. My research focuses on extending biological understanding through experimental-computational synergy. I developed methods to disentangle cellular identities in single-cell data and enable cross-modality prediction, as well as tools for learning from live measurements using visual reasoning.

I earned my PhD in computer science at the Hebrew University of Jerusalem with Mor Nitzan, developing methods for decoding cellular identities in single-cell data. During my PhD I interned with Marco Cuturi at Apple ML Research in Paris, working on representation learning. I completed an MSc in physics at the Hebrew University of Jerusalem with Naftali Tishby and Zohar Ringel, studying information theory.
